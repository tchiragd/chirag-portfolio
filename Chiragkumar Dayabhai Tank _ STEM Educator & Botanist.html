About Me

Who I Am

I am a dedicated educator and science professional with a unique blend of experience in botany, environmental science, and advanced STEM education. With a strong academic foundation in Botany and a B.Ed. qualification, I bring both subject expertise and pedagogical skill to everything I do.

My passion lies in making complex concepts accessible — whether teaching foundational literacy and numeracy to primary students or guiding them through advanced robotics and AI. I believe every child deserves education that connects to their world.

I actively localize educational content in Gujarati script, creating manuals, video scripts, and student assessment papers to ensure accessible learning for local communities across Gujarat.
